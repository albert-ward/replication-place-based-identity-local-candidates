\section{1. Observational survey: \texttt{ward\_jop\_yougov\_data.sav}}
% =========================================================================

\emph{Source.} YouGov UniOM omnibus, University of Oxford. Online survey of the YouGov panel, sampled to be representative of adults in Great Britain (18+). Fieldwork 21--22 August 2022. $N$ = 1{,}793. Analysis variables only. The remaining omnibus variables are not released.

\emph{Missing values.} Codes 8/98 (``Skipped'') and 9/99 (``Not Asked'') follow YouGov conventions. ``Don't know'' is coded 6 on the attitude items and is treated as missing in the analysis.

\section{2. Survey experiment: \texttt{ward\_jop\_localcandidates\_exp\_data.csv}}
% =========================================================================

\emph{Source.} Qualtrics survey fielded to US adults through Prolific, 16--18 April 2025. Preregistered at \href{https://osf.io/mwbcu}{osf.io/mwbcu}. One row per participant ($N$ = 4{,}803 recruited, before exclusions). Conjoint attributes were randomized in Qualtrics with JavaScript (\texttt{conjoint\_code\_v2.txt}).

\emph{De-identification.} Prolific identifiers, ZIP codes, timestamps and unused platform variables were removed before release (\texttt{ward\_jop\_data\_preparation.R}). ZIP codes were first used to derive state and RUCA code.

\emph{Missing values.} Conjoint variables are NA for rounds a participant did not reach, and \texttt{manipulation\_check\_1} and \texttt{attention\_check} are NA for 14 participants who did not answer them. Personal details in the free-text reflections are replaced with bracketed tags (see \texttt{treatment}).

\emph{Exclusions.} The sample after exclusions omits participants who completed in under 60 seconds or more than three standard deviations above the mean duration, have \texttt{gpt\_answer} $\neq$ 0, have \texttt{short\_text} = 1, have \texttt{Q\_RecaptchaScore} $\leq$ 0.4, or did not answer ``Strongly disagree'' to \texttt{attention\_check}.

\section{3. Pilot study: \texttt{ward\_jop\_pilot\_data.csv}}

Participants received the treatment or control vignette, followed immediately by the manipulation check. Timestamps were removed before release, and one place name in the reflections is replaced with [town].
